Pantheon of gods worshipped in [[The New World]]
They were originally rulers of [[The Old Empire]] but ascended to godhood in [[The Ritual]]

[[Mokosh]]
- goddess of fertility, birth and rain
[[Perun]]
- god of thunder and lightning
[[Veles]]
- god of death and the underworld as well as wealth
[[Svarog]]
- god of the sun
[[Hokra]]
- goddess of war and victory
[[Devana]]
- god of the wilds and undisturbed nature
[[Setesh]]
- god of grain, civilization but also winter and decay
[[Triglav]]
- god of knowledge and magic
[[Zorya]]
- goddess of change, dawn and the moon
