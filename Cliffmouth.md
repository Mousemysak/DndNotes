A quickly growing town on the new continent. It was originally less popular than it's neighbor southport due to perceived danger from kobolds, but since a deal has been made with them a few years ago, Cliffmouth has been quickly growing as the prime exporter of ore to the empire and a hub for adventurers, explorers and the like.
