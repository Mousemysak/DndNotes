Used to be a kingdom in the northern part of the Shattered Isles, however it broke apart after the death of its last ruler Armundus Lastien.
Armundus was a wise and generaly pretty noble king, who generaly disliked the Emperor. He planned to revolt against the emperor and make the kingoms of the iron empire indepentent again, however the emperor caught wind of his plans, and had him secretly killed.  Armundus didn't leave behind an obvious heir, which the emperor used to his advantage. He pitted Lastien's nephew Bralis against his grandson Benic, which resulted in the Lastien civil war. Bralis, who was a minor noble in the nort parth of Lastien tried to storm the capital city, but Benic's forces repelled the attack. Since then the war has not seen much progress, as both sides hold out their easily defendable positions.
