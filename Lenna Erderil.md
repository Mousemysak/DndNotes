A young elven female soldier in the Recon division, she is tasked with looking over and commanding Recon units 150 to 175 including [[The Party|Reconnaissance Unit #163]]

Raised in South Lastien, she wanted to free her country and unite it with north lastien, thus joining the army. She was a skilled tactician and planner so she quickly rose in rank, however, upon noticing her skill, the Empire sent her off to the new world to monitor and manage exploration and conflict with locals

She is quite energetic and is willing to take on huge amounts of work, devoted to the empire and the army, and generally quite kind to those around her.
